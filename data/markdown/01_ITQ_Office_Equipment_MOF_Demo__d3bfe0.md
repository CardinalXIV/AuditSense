**MINISTRY OF FINANCE**
**INVITATION TO QUOTE (ITQ)**
**FOR THE SUPPLY AND DELIVERY OF ERGONOMIC OFFICE CHAIRS AND ADJUSTABLE DESKS**
**ITQ Reference No.: MOF/ITQ/2025/015**
**Agency:**  Ministry of Finance (MOF), Corporate Services Division
**Issue Date:**  2 January 2026

---

## **1. Background**
The Ministry of Finance (MOF) Corporate Services Division requires the supply and delivery of ergonomic office chairs and height-adjustable desks for staff located at the MOF Main Office at 100 Parliament Place, Singapore 179000.
This Invitation to Quote (ITQ) seeks quotations from qualified suppliers to provide, deliver and install the required furniture in accordance with the specifications and requirements set out in this document and its Annexes.

---

## **2. Scope of Requirement**
The Successful Contractor shall:
1.​ Supply and deliver new ergonomic office chairs and height-adjustable desks. 2.​ Provide delivery, assembly and installation at MOF premises. 3.​ Provide on-site product briefing to appointed MOF representatives. 4.​ Provide warranty and after-sales support in accordance with Clause 7.

---

## **3. Items Required**

|  Item No.  |  Description  |  Estimated Quantity  |
|---:|:---|:---|
|  1  | Ergonomic Office Chair  (mesh back)  | 120 units  |
|  2  | Height-Adjustable Desk  (electric, 120cm) | 80 units  |

*The quantities are indicative and may be subject to variation at MOF’s discretion.*
Detailed technical requirements are set out in  **Annex A -  Technical Specifications**  and the pricing format is provided in  **Annex B -  Pricing Schedule**  .

---

## **4. Contract Period**
The expected contract period is:
- ​  **Contract Start Date:**    *1 February 2026*

- ​  **Completion Date:**  31 January 2027
(See  **Clause 5.2**  for delivery schedule.)

---

## **5. Delivery and Installation**
5.1 The Successful Contractor shall complete delivery and installation of all items within  **8 weeks**  from the date of the Letter of Award (LOA).
5.2 Partial deliveries are allowed, subject to prior approval by the MOF Officer-in-Charge.

---

## **6. Submission of Quotations**
6.1 Vendors shall submit their quotations via the designated Government procurement system by:
- ​  **Closing Date:**  16 January 2026

- ​  **Closing Time:**  4:00 p.m. (Singapore Time)
6.2 Late submissions will not be considered.
6.3 Vendors shall submit the following documents:
- ​ Completed  **Annex B -  Pricing Schedule**

- ​ Product brochures or datasheets

- ​ Company ACRA profile or equivalent

- ​ At least two (2) reference projects of similar nature

---

## **7. Warranty and Support**
7.1 The Successful Contractor shall provide a  **minimum warranty of three (3) years**  on all supplied items covering manufacturing defects, structural failures and mechanical defects.
7.2 Response time for warranty claims shall not exceed  **three (3) working days**  upon notification.

---

## **8. Evaluation Criteria**
Quotations will be evaluated based on but not limited to the following criteria:
- ​ Compliance with technical specifications

- ​ Total evaluated price (based on Annex B)

- ​ Delivery schedule and warranty support

- ​ Past performance and relevant experience

---

## **9. Clarifications**
All clarifications shall be submitted via the procurement system using the ITQ reference **MOF/ITQ/2025/015**  no later than  **10 January 2026**  .
